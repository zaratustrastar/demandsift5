# T14 — access-safe partial results

Implemented September 1, 2026 in the isolated speed-up branch. Production remains unchanged; `SCAN_PARTIAL_RESULTS=0` until the T16 dashboard and rollout gates are complete.

## Outcome

Each scan can now own a bounded, checkpointed `partialResults` snapshot with a monotonically increasing version. It contains three deliberately separate classes:

1. `candidate_preview`: relevance screening succeeded and `qualificationStatus` is explicitly `pending`. It is never called a lead or potential customer.
2. `potential_customer` or `relevant_conversation`: deep qualification and the existing evidence/publishing gates succeeded.
3. `reply`: a draft is pending, ready, or failed with a safe code; provider error content is never exposed.

The store is additive JSON in the existing scan record; no schema migration or architecture replacement was needed. Current entries are a bounded snapshot, not an unbounded event log. Up to 1,000 recent tombstones are retained as invalidation hints.

## Identity and recovery

- Preview, qualified-result, and reply IDs are deterministic hashes of scan and source identity. They reveal no prompts or credentials.
- Repeating the same checkpoint is a no-op: version does not advance and cards/replies do not duplicate.
- A richer replacement keeps the stable card ID and receives a newer item version. Removed or superseded previews get tombstones.
- The final-authoritative triage pool publishes previews only after reconciliation. Qualification atomically replaces all pending previews with the qualified snapshot.
- Partial results remain on a failed/retrying scan; the failure path never clears them.
- `runtimeProgress.partialResultsVersion` and `timing.firstResultAt` update with the first visible checkpoint.

Stable IDs also replace random opportunity/intelligence/reply IDs in final assembly. This makes retry/reload reuse deterministic without changing selection, ranking, coverage, or evidence rules.

## Endpoint and access

`GET /api/scans/:scanId/partial?afterVersion=N`:

- authenticates the workspace and returns 404 for another workspace;
- rejects invalid/non-integer versions;
- returns `{ changed: false, version }` when the caller is current;
- otherwise returns an access-filtered full snapshot plus newer tombstones;
- uses `Cache-Control: private, no-store`;
- loads only `websiteUrl` and `partialResults` from PostgreSQL, not the final report, crawl, raw checkpoints, or provider failures.

The partial presenter reuses the final presenter's opportunity, relevant-conversation, reply, and free/full visibility logic. Internal fingerprints and raw records are stripped. Counts are labeled `foundSoFar`, and `complete` is always false; final totals remain exclusive to the coverage-checked report.

## Workflow publication points

- Screened previews are persisted after exact triage coverage and final candidate reconciliation.
- Qualified/relevant results are persisted immediately after global aggregation and evidence gates, before insights and replies.
- Ready replies are added after validation and durable reply storage.

T15 moves reply checkpoints to each item and overlaps tail work. T16 consumes versions in the live dashboard.
