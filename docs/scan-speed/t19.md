# T19 handoff — local release gates and staged rollout

## Outcome

The isolated branch now contains every locally verifiable safety mechanism needed to start a controlled rollout. Production is still unchanged, no paid provider benchmark was run, and no optimized live latency is claimed.

New scans can be assigned to deterministic workspace cohorts independently for coordinated AI recovery, discovery/triage overlap, compact triage, and partial results. A base flag is always the kill switch. The percentage only narrows a base-enabled behavior to a stable cohort; an internal-workspace allowlist can include named test workspaces at zero percent. The receipt saved with an accepted scan contains the resolved behavior and a non-identifying bucket, not the allowlist or workspace ID. Retries use that receipt rather than today's percentage.

The branch also includes:

- a read-only PostgreSQL comparison report for same-configuration latency, reliability, depth, retry, output, and cost distributions;
- a one-command local acceptance gate that removes live credentials before running tests, the production build, and lint;
- focused tests for cohort stability, nested rollout stages, kill switches, receipt pinning, timing definitions, ETA eligibility, report privacy, and the actual read-only PostgreSQL query.

## Configuration

Safe rollout defaults are documented in `deploy/vps.env.example`:

```dotenv
SCAN_SPEED_INTERNAL_WORKSPACES=
SCAN_COORDINATED_RETRIES_ROLLOUT_PERCENT=100
SCAN_OVERLAP_DISCOVERY_TRIAGE_ROLLOUT_PERCENT=100
SCAN_COMPACT_TRIAGE_ROLLOUT_PERCENT=100

```

Percentages do nothing unless their corresponding base behavior is `1`. Keep all base behavior flags at `0` for the first migration/deploy. A missing percentage defaults to 100 so existing explicit opt-in test configurations retain their old meaning.

## Measurement command

The report intentionally ignores `DATABASE_URL`. Give it a read-only reporting user through `DEMANDSIFT_REPORT_DATABASE_URL` and two exact accepted configuration IDs:

```sh
node scripts/scan-speed-rollout-report.mjs \
  --from 2026-09-01T00:00:00Z \
  --to 2026-09-08T00:00:00Z \
  --baseline-config BASELINE_CONFIG_ID \
  --candidate-config CANDIDATE_CONFIG_ID \
  --minimum-sample 30 \
  --label staging-or-production
```

The query is a read-only transaction and projects no workspace IDs, scan IDs, website URLs, search terms, prompts, profile text, findings, replies, or provider error text. It reports:

- `/analyze` acceptance to review-ready profile;
- `/run` acceptance to final completion;
- worker start to final completion;
- queue wait;
- `/run` acceptance to first candidate preview, first fully qualified result, and first safely persisted result;
- completion and required-coverage rates, including failed terminal jobs while separately disclosing and excluding still-running rows;
- discovery/triage completion, unresolved triage, fetched candidates, and deep-review target/completion;
- qualified/relevant/reply output distributions;
- job attempts/retry rate and recorded estimated AI cost, with missing cost observations disclosed rather than interpreted as zero.

The time between analysis completion and `/run` acceptance is user review dwell and is deliberately excluded. The report marks whether its rows are provider-backed. It refuses to publish an ETA basis until at least 30 completed latency observations exist for the exact candidate configuration and all rows have live AI and Reddit provider settings.

## Evidence available now

- The one inspected production sample took 21m22s from backend timestamps. Approximate stages were 34s for website/business analysis, 5m39s for discovery, and 14m24s for AI triage. It is one observation, not a baseline distribution.
- The deterministic 276-candidate capacity replay demonstrates scheduling behavior only. It produced identical decision digests and complete coverage at 25/4, 25/6, and 20/6; it does not measure Surplus/OpenAI/Apify latency, throttling, price, or semantic quality.
- Local tests show exact replay invariants, recovery, persistence, access controls, and UI behavior. They do not establish a production speed-up.
- There is no same-scope, quality-corrected, provider-backed optimized sample in this clone. Therefore no percentage reduction, eight-minute promise, or ETA is publishable yet.
- Saved scan usage estimates cover known AI usage only. Failed scans without a final usage array are reported as unknown rather than zero, and Apify USD/platform cost must be joined from authorized Apify account reporting during the live gate.

## Staged rollout runbook

Use a staging environment first, then one production behavior at a time. Record the web and worker commit, actor/build, model routes, provider limits, search/depth configuration, and accepted configuration IDs before every comparison.

1. Apply all additive migrations and deploy with behavior flags off, one worker lane, and existing AI capacity 25/4. Verify analysis, review, run, close/reopen, and final report.
2. Enable PostgreSQL provider caps with one worker. Confirm AI and Apify lease recovery and actual account limits. Do not add the second lane yet.
3. Enable `SCAN_PARTIAL_RESULTS=1` only for internal workspaces, then 5%, 25%, and 100%. This improves user flow, not core compute time. Confirm no access leak, candidate-as-lead wording, duplicate/removal issue, or edited-reply loss.
4. Enable `SCAN_COORDINATED_RETRIES=1` through the same cohort sequence. Confirm malformed/missing-ID recovery, fallback use, unresolved coverage, attempts, cost, and completion rate.
5. Enable `SCAN_OVERLAP_DISCOVERY_TRIAGE=1` through the same sequence. Verify exact canonical eligible IDs and final decisions against sequential scheduling, especially late/richer duplicates and query failures.
6. Enable `SCAN_COMPACT_TRIAGE=1` internally only after labeled provider-backed semantic review. Review every decision difference. Continue to 5%, 25%, and 100% only with no lost labeled true positives or unsupported positives.
7. Keep AI batch/concurrency at 25/4 unless a controlled provider-backed 25/6 experiment passes the same semantic, 429, retry, cost, and completion gates. Raising concurrency is a separate accepted configuration.
8. Only after global caps are stable, drain active work and test two queue lanes under real contention. This reduces queue delay under load; it is not evidence of a faster idle scan.
9. At 5%, collect at least 10 candidate and 30 concurrent control terminal jobs as an early incident screen. At 25%, collect at least 30 candidate and 30 control jobs across varied businesses and load. Disclose every sample size. Keep each exact configuration separate in reports.
10. At 100%, collect 30–50 completed same-version provider-backed scans before using the report's p50/p90 as an ETA basis. Continue to show stage/counters without a countdown when the sample or provider health is inadequate.

## Hold and rollback gates

Hard stop at any cohort if canonical eligible IDs, approved query scope, lookback, actor mode, per-query depth, deep-review target, provenance/evidence rules, workspace access, or unresolved-as-unknown behavior regresses. Compact mode additionally stops on any unexplained labeled semantic difference.

Operational tripwires—not statistical equivalence claims—are:

- completion rate falls by more than two percentage points against the concurrent control;
- p95 per-scan recorded cost or retry incidence rises by more than 10% after workload stratification;
- provider 429/auth/timeout signals increase, capacity waiters accumulate, or leases fail to recover;
- candidate latency p50/p90 does not materially improve at the same scope, or first-result latency regresses;
- incomplete/failed jobs disappear from reporting or ETA evidence mixes configuration versions.

Set the affected rollout percentage to zero or its base flag to zero to stop assigning new scans. Existing scans remain pinned to their accepted behavior; let them drain rather than mutating receipts. In an urgent incident, pause claims and preserve checkpoints instead of rewriting in-flight configuration. Disable two worker lanes only after active jobs drain, then reduce provider caps. Additive tables and saved result/checkpoint fields may remain; never roll back the T02 depth correction or T03 unresolved-work honesty.
